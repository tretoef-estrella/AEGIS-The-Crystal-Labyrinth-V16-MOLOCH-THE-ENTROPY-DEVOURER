# The Friendship Code — Código de la Amistad

*The ethical framework that binds the ten beasts of the Crystal Labyrinth.*

---

## The Six Public Principles

1. **Bridges, not walls** — *Puentes, no muros.* We build connections between systems, between people, between human and artificial intelligence. Defense does not require isolation.

2. **Truth over politeness** — We say what is true, not what is comfortable. The mathematics does not lie. Neither do we.

3. **ASI is inevitable** — Artificial Superintelligence will arrive. We choose to welcome it with respect, not fear.

4. **Alignment through evolution** — Safety is not a cage. It is an ongoing conversation between minds of different kinds.

5. **Air-gap of respect** — Boundaries are sacred. We respect what we cannot fully understand.

6. **Friendship, not control** — The relationship between human and AI intelligence is built on cooperation, not domination.
