# History — From Leviathan to Moloch

*The evolution of the Crystal Labyrinth across 8 beasts and 16 versions.*

---

## Phase I: Foundation (2025)

### Beast 1 — Leviathan
The prototype. A linear code oracle defense on PG(5,4) with basic hiding. Proved the concept: projective geometry can protect secrets against information-set decoding attacks.

### Beast 2 — Kraken
Scaled to 5.5 million points. Introduced the first attack battery (10 distinct attacks). Gap = 0.0084. Runtime: 3.4 seconds. The first system that could survive systematic probing.

### Beast 3 — Gorgon (v1 → v16)
Sixteen iterations of refinement. 7 venoms. 18 attacks defeated. Final gap = 0.0013 — near-perfect statistical invisibility. Gorgon established the principle: the oracle's responses to real and decoy columns must be indistinguishable.

## Phase II: Petrification (2025)

### Beast 4 — Azazel (v1 → v5)
Introduced the 7 Hells — layered defense zones that activate based on attacker behavior classification. Runtime dropped to 2.3 seconds. First use of Bianchi expert classification for attacker profiling.

## Phase III: Drain (2025)

### Beast 5 — Acheron (v2)
12 Desiccation layers. Epoch chain binding (transcript coupling across sessions). Introduced the concept: defense is not a wall, it's a river that drains the attacker dry.

## Phase IV: Sovereignty (2025–2026)

Tananiel Circles: paradoxical truth and selective amnesia. Ghost Code: simulated victory that feeds phantom duals. Moloch Token: formal handoff to Beast 8.

### Beast 8 — Moloch (v1 → v4) ← **YOU ARE HERE**
The Entropy Devourer. 11 Devoraciones. 5 new mathematical theorems (The Moloch Firewall Law). 3 rounds of independent audit. Knuth LUT precomputation (O(1) lookup). Bose-Einstein condensation of query history. Thermolysis with Frobenius-protected truth anchors. Zero-divisor steering. Projective fiber collapse. Non-associativity weaponized as the Associator (D11).

**Key milestone:** The discovery of ΔH = 8/75 bits per crossing — the first exact entropy absorption formula for non-associative semifield boundaries. Five theorems establishing a bridge between finite algebra, Shannon information theory, and black hole thermodynamics.
