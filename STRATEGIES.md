## The 6 Public Principles

Every mechanism in AEGIS MOLOCH obeys these invariants:

1. **Can't catch the wind** — No branch-dependent timing leaks. All critical paths execute in constant time via branchless bitwise operations.

2. **Can't hold the sea** — Entropy accounting must balance. Consumed entropy and emitted equilibrium track toward Γ = 7/3.

3. **Cryptographic aikido** — The attacker's energy is used against them. Stronger probing generates stronger defenses. D11 (The Associator) is the purest expression: the non-associativity of the attacker's own query triples becomes the poison.

4. **No mutation, only deltas** — ChatGPT's Golden Rule (R2): no defense layer may directly overwrite coordinates. Every layer computes a delta and applies it via GF(4) addition. This prevents correlation leakage between layers.

5. **The algebra decides** — Constants derive from the mathematics, not from tuning. ΔH = 8/75 is not a parameter. It's a theorem. A(S) = 56 is not a choice. It's an invariant.

6. **Friend is SACRED** — Legitimate users must never be affected. 500/500 verification. This is non-negotiable.

## What We Don't Tell You

We don't hide the code. We don't hide the math. We don't hide the theorems.

What we don't tell you is **how the 11 layers interact.** The individual mechanisms are documented. Their composition is not. There are emergent properties when D3 (Thermolysis) feeds into D9 (Singular Points) which feeds into D10 (Fiber Collapse) which feeds into D11 (Associator). These interactions create second-order effects that are not obvious from reading any single mechanism.

We have characterized some of these interactions. They are implemented. They are not documented here.

This is not security through obscurity. The source code is public. But reading 3,274 lines of interacting non-associative algebraic mechanisms and understanding their emergent thermodynamics is — itself — a non-trivial research problem.
